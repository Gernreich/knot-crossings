Each rope lifts toward you where it goes over and drops away where it goes under, as the drawing’s circles say. The 1st rope is red, the 2nd blue. Loose is the rope in the hand. Joined own closes each rope on itself (a link) and joined across runs one rope into the other (a knot), the two closures the main page names; the joining rope is grey.
